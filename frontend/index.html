Professional Communication & Ethics Charter

1. Cooperation Over Blame

The platform frames waste management as a shared civic responsibility between citizens and civic bodies rather than an adversarial surveillance system.

2. Sanitation Worker Dignity

Avoids punitive or degrading tracking of individual sanitary workers. Emphasizes operational dispatch, equipment allocation, and constructive resolution.

3. Privacy & Minimal Personal Data

Camera access and geolocation permissions are fully optional. Phone numbers and personal resident identifiers are shielded from public display.

4. Transparency & Honest Representation

Sample data, simulated municipal dispatches, and illustrative coupon points are explicitly labeled so users and academic evaluators understand the prototype's exact scope.
